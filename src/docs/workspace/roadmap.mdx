The roadmap is `/roadmap`. It is public. It has three lanes:

| Lane | Status | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planned | Planned | What you intend to do next |
| In progress | In Progress | What is being built now |
| Complete | Complete | What recently shipped |

Open suggestions stay on the feedback board. They are not a promise, so they are not on the roadmap. Closed suggestions are off the roadmap too. Closing something is how you say it will not be done, or that it is no longer relevant.

## Cards

Each card shows the board, the title, a short excerpt of the description, the author, the vote count, and up to three tags. The vote button on the card is the same vote as on the board. Changing status in the workspace moves the card to another lane. You do not drag cards on the roadmap.

The lanes include every board. The board name on the card is how you tell them apart.

## When a card appears

Move the suggestion to Planned, In Progress, or Complete in the workspace. The roadmap updates from that status. There is no separate "publish to roadmap" switch.

If assignment is Automatic, the moment you set Planned also assigns the next staff member. That assignment is not printed on the public card. See [Statuses and assignment](/workspace/statuses).
