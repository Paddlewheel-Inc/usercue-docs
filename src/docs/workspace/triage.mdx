Votes tell you what people want. Triage adds what it would cost and what it would change, so the next thing you build is a decision and not a guess. Everything here is staff only. Customers never see impact, effort, scores, or notes.

## Impact and effort

On a suggestion, staff with Manage posts can set two values. Each has five levels, from Very low (1) to Very high (5), or Not set.

| Field | Higher means |
| --- | --- |
| Impact | More value to customers or to you |
| Effort | More work to build |

Either value can be cleared again. The scale is yours to define, so agree on what a 3 means before the team starts scoring.

## Priority score

When both values are set, UserCue calculates one number:

```text
priority = (votes + 1) × impact ÷ effort
```

The result is rounded to one decimal. The `+ 1` keeps a brand new suggestion with no votes from scoring zero.

| Votes | Impact | Effort | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 5 | 1 | 5.0 |
| 12 | 4 | 2 | 26.0 |
| 40 | 2 | 5 | 16.4 |

A suggestion missing either value has no score and sorts last under Priority. Choose Priority score in the Sort by menu on the [suggestion list](/workspace/filters). The sort is hidden when triage is off everywhere. It is never offered on the public board, and a request for it from a non-staff visitor falls back to Top.

## Internal notes

Notes are a private thread on each suggestion. Use them for context a customer should not see: who asked, links to the support ticket, why it was declined.

- Every staff member can read and add notes. Notes are plain text, up to 2000 characters.
- You can delete your own notes. Staff with Manage posts can delete anyone's.
- Notes move with a suggestion if it is [merged](/boards/duplicates).

## Turn triage off

Some teams only want votes and a roadmap. Triage can be disabled for the whole workspace, or kept on for specific boards.

In [General settings](/settings/general#triage-tools):

- **Triage tools** is the master switch. With it off, the Triage panel is hidden everywhere.
- With it on, choose All boards, or pick the boards that use it. Suggestions on other boards have no triage panel.

When triage is off for a suggestion, its values and notes are kept but not shown. Turn it on again and they reappear. Over the API, the triage endpoints return `404` for a suggestion where triage is off, and setting `impact` or `effort` returns `400`.

## AI assist

If [AI assist](/integrations/ai) is configured, the triage panel can summarize a long thread and suggest tags. Both only fill in text or checkboxes for you to confirm.
