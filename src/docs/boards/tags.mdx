Tags are short labels on a suggestion, such as Bug, Billing, or iOS. They are for scanning a list. They do not change status, and they do not put a suggestion on the roadmap.

## Create and edit

![Tag color picker in Settings](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-13-at-9.41.52-PM@2x.png "Tag color picker in Settings")

Open Settings, then Tags. You need Manage posts or Workspace settings.

Type a name, up to 24 characters, pick a color, and choose Add tag. Names are unique ignoring case. `Bug` and `bug` are the same tag.

Colors are a fixed set: pink, rose, coral, amber, lime, green, teal, sky, blue, violet, and stone. There is no custom color picker. The dot on the tag uses the color you picked, separate from the workspace accent, so a green tag stays green if you later switch the accent to violet.

Click the name to rename it. Color opens the same swatches. Delete asks you to confirm, then removes the tag from every suggestion that had it.

Renaming or recoloring updates the suggestions that already use the tag. You do not reapply them.

## Apply a tag

On a suggestion, staff with Manage posts see Tag next to the tags already on it. The menu lists every tag. Choose one to add it, or choose it again to take it off. A suggestion can hold 8 tags. After that, tags it does not already have are disabled until you remove one.

If the menu says there are no tags yet, create them in Settings first. The public suggestion shows the tags and does not show the Tag button.

Tags also show on the board list, the workspace inbox, roadmap cards, and the newest cards on Overview. Long names truncate inside the card instead of stretching the row.
