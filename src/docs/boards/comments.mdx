## Votes

Each visitor gets one vote per suggestion. Selecting the vote again removes it. The count on the card and on the suggestion updates immediately.

The vote is tied to the browser, not only to an account. A signed-in person and a guest on the same browser share that vote. Top on the public board sorts by this count, then by newest.

Staff do not get a separate vote weight. A staff vote counts the same as anyone else's.

## Comments

Comments sit at the bottom of a suggestion. A comment can be up to 2,000 characters and uses the same editor as the description, including `#` links to other suggestions.

Who can comment follows the same guest rule as posting. If guests are off, the comment box asks the person to sign in. A suspended account cannot comment.

Staff comments show the role badge and a ring on the avatar, so a reply from the team is visible without a separate "staff said" label.

The number next to Comments includes replies.

## Replies

Reply sits on the comment, not in a separate box at the bottom. Choosing it opens a composer directly under that comment. Cancel closes it. Choosing Reply again on the same comment also closes it.

The reply is stored on that comment. It shows underneath, oldest first, with a line marking the thread. A reply to a reply nests under that reply, not back at the top of the suggestion. After a few levels the indent stops growing so the thread does not run off the page, but the replies still stay in order under the comment they answered.

The composer at the very bottom starts a new top-level comment. It does not attach to the thread you had open.

## What comments are not

Comments do not change status. Status changes are the activity lines above the thread. A comment is also not a vote, and deleting a suggestion removes the whole thread with it.
