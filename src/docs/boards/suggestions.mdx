A suggestion is one idea on a board. The public list shows the title, author, date, vote count, and any tags. Opening it shows the description, status, comments, and, for staff, the controls to change it.

## Posting

Choose the board, then write a title and an optional description.

- Titles can be up to 140 characters.
- Descriptions can be up to 8,000 characters. The editor supports bold, italic, underline, highlight, links, and images. Images are limited to 1.5 MB.
- In the description or a comment, type `#` to link another suggestion. The link shows up as a chip that opens that post.
- While you type the title, UserCue shows existing suggestions that look like yours, so people can vote for one instead of posting a duplicate. See [Similar posts and merging](/boards/duplicates).

A new suggestion starts as Open. The author is the signed-in name, or the name a guest typed. Posting also counts as the author's vote.

## Guests

General settings has Allow guests to post feedback without signing in.

On means someone can post and comment with a display name and no account. Off means they have to sign in with a connected provider before the form will submit. Staff still post as themselves either way.

Guest posting does not create a workspace login. It only attaches the name they typed to that suggestion.

## What staff can change

Manage posts covers the suggestion itself:

- Status, from the menu on the suggestion.
- Title and description.
- Tags, up to 8. See [Tags](/boards/tags).
- Assignee, only when assignment is set to Manual. See [Statuses and assignment](/workspace/statuses).
- Impact, effort, and internal notes, when [triage tools](/workspace/triage) are on.
- Merge into another suggestion, to fold a duplicate together. See [Similar posts and merging](/boards/duplicates).
- Delete. Deleting a suggestion deletes its votes, comments, and status history.

The status row on the suggestion is only the status. It does not say who moved it. That record is the activity line under the suggestion: the staff avatar, their name, and the status they set. The original Open event is not repeated there, because the suggestion already shows who posted it.

## Shipped

When a [changelog entry](/changelog) is linked to a suggestion, the suggestion shows a Shipped in badge that opens the entry. People who voted on it are told, if [voter alerts](/settings/notifications#voter-alerts) are on.

## Where suggestions show up

- The board list, sorted by Top (votes) or New.
- The workspace inbox, with search, status, tag, assignee, and vote filters, plus [saved views](/workspace/filters).
- Your own site, through an [embed](/integrations/embeds).
- Overview, in the newest cards and in On stage when the status is In Progress.
- The roadmap, once the status is Planned, In Progress, or Complete.
