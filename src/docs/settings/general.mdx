General holds the rules that decide how people use your board day to day. You need Workspace settings to open it. Every switch here saves as soon as you select it. The vote limit has a Save button, because it is a number you type.

Looking for color or the logo? That is Branding. Looking for email? That is [Notifications](/settings/notifications). Staff invites are on the [Team](/settings/staff) tab.

## Feedback

Allow guests to post feedback without signing in.

On: a visitor types a name and can post a suggestion or a comment. No account is created.

Off: the form refuses until they sign in with a provider you have enabled. If no provider is enabled, they see that sign-in is required and cannot post. Staff are unaffected. They are already signed in.

This switch does not delete old guest posts. It only changes what new posts and comments require. It also applies to the [embed widget](/integrations/embeds).

## Changelog

Three independent switches control the [changelog](/changelog).

| Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Publish the changelog | Shows the Changelog link in the public footer and makes entries public. Off hides it from customers but keeps your entries. |
| Allow comments on updates | Lets signed-in people comment on an entry |
| Allow reactions on updates | Shows the Up, Party, Heart, and Rocket reactions |

Comments and reactions keep their setting if you hide the changelog. When the changelog is on, the page shows the [RSS](/changelog#rss) address to copy.

## Assignment

![Assignment mode selector in Settings](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-13-at-9.39.17-PM@2x.png "Assignment mode selector in Settings")

This is the workspace-wide rule for who is responsible for a suggestion. The three modes are Off, Manual, and Automatic. What each one does on a suggestion is written up in [Statuses and assignment](/workspace/statuses).

Short version:

- Off hides assignment. Nobody can be assigned.
- Manual lets staff pick a person from the people who can open the workspace and are not suspended.
- Automatic assigns the next person in that same list when a suggestion becomes Planned, then moves to the following person the next time.

Switching modes does not clear assignees already set.

## Triage tools

Impact and effort scoring, the priority sort, and internal notes on each suggestion. Only staff ever see them. See [Triage tools](/workspace/triage) for how they work.

- **Show triage tools to staff** is the master switch. Off hides everything triage-related, everywhere.
- When it is on and you have more than one board, you can choose which boards use triage. Suggestions on a board that is not ticked have no triage panel.

Turning triage off keeps the data. Scores and notes come back if you turn it on again.

## Voting

Limit how many open suggestions one person can back at once. The default is 0, which means unlimited. The maximum is 100.

- Only active suggestions count: Open, Planned, and In Progress. Votes come back automatically when a suggestion ships or closes, so people are never stuck.
- The count follows the person across devices when they are signed in. A guest is counted by browser.
- Someone at the limit sees "You are out of votes" when they try to vote, and the vote is refused. They can still remove a vote and use it elsewhere.
- Staff and the API follow the same limit.
- Lowering the limit does not remove votes people already cast. It only blocks new ones.

A limit makes votes a scarce signal, which helps when everything ends up with a vote. Developers can read a visitor's budget from the [API](/api#vote-budget).
