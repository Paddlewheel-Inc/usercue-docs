Users is the list of registered accounts. You need Manage users. Guests who posted with only a name are not in this list, because they never created an account.

Each row is a person: name, email, avatar, role, and when they joined. New faces on Overview is the same list, shortened to the latest few, and it links back here.

## Roles on an account

The role on the row is what they can do.

- Owner has every permission. The role is locked. The last owner cannot be suspended, moved to another role, or deleted.
- Staff can open the workspace, manage suggestions, and manage boards.
- Member is the default for someone who signed in on the public board. Members have no workspace permissions.
- Any other role you created on [Roles](/settings/roles) shows up in the same menu.

Changing the role takes effect the next time their session is checked. They do not need a new password.

## Suspend

Suspend stops that account from posting, commenting, and using staff actions. They can still sign in and see the error. A suspended person is also skipped by automatic assignment, and they disappear from the manual assignee list.

Unsuspend restores the account. It does not replay anything they tried to do while suspended.

## Delete

Delete removes the account and signs them out. Suggestions they authored stay on the board, with the name that was saved on the post. Their votes and sessions go with the account.

Deleting is not the same as removing staff. Remove staff only takes the workspace role away. Delete removes the account.
