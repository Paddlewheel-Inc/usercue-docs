UserCue is a public board for suggestions and a private workspace for the people who triage them.

Visitors open the board, post an idea, vote, and comment. Staff open the workspace, move suggestions through status, assign them, tag them, and publish a roadmap of what is planned, in progress, and done.

The two sides share one workspace. The accent color, boards, tags, and sign-in methods are workspace settings. Light and dark mode is a personal choice stored in the browser.

## The public board

The board is what customers see.

- [Boards](/boards) group suggestions. A workspace can have more than one, such as Feature requests and Bugs.
- A [suggestion](/boards/suggestions) is a title, an optional description, votes, comments, a status, and any tags staff have applied.
- The [roadmap](/workspace/roadmap) shows Planned, In Progress, and Complete. Open and Closed stay off the roadmap.

Anyone can read the board. Posting and commenting follow the guest setting in [General settings](/settings/general). With guests allowed, a name is enough. With guests turned off, the person has to sign in first.

## The workspace

Staff with the Dashboard permission open `/admin`.

- [Overview](/workspace) is the home page: counts by status, what is in progress, and the newest suggestions.
- Requests is the inbox. Filter by board and status, search, and open a suggestion to change it.
- [Settings](/settings) covers color, guests, assignment, invite email, tags, sign-in, users, and roles. Each tab is limited to the permission that owns it.

The first account on a new workspace is the owner. That role is locked and has every permission. Later people join as staff from an invite, or as members when they sign in on the public board.
